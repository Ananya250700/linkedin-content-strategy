# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-05

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Why wellness blogs drive traffic but not demo requests — and the three-question filter that closes the gap

---

Your wellness blog gets thousands of visits. Your demo request form gets silence.

That's not an SEO problem. It's a content strategy problem.

Most wellness content is built to rank — generic guides on "how to reduce employee burnout" that answer the search query but never speak to the person actually buying the software.

Here's the gap I see consistently: top-of-funnel content dominates wellness content calendars. Broad awareness topics, high search volume, easy to produce. Meanwhile, the blogs that actually convert — case studies, decision frameworks, comparison guides, ROI calculators — barely exist.

The result? Traffic from people who'll never buy, and nothing for the CHRO who's already sold on wellness but needs to justify the spend internally.

Three questions before publishing any wellness piece:
1. Who is searching for this — and are they a buyer?
2. Does this help someone make a decision, or just educate them?
3. Is there a next step that moves them closer to a demo?

If your wellness content can't answer all three, it's working for Google — not for your pipeline.

What does your wellness content calendar actually optimize for?
