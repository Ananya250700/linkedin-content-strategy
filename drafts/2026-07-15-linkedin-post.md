# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-15

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most wellness vendors are publishing for awareness, not for the buyer who's actually close to a decision.

---

Most wellness content teams are building the top of a funnel nobody's finishing.

The average wellness platform publishes 20+ awareness-level blogs — "why employee wellness matters," "benefits of a wellness program" — and calls it a content strategy.

HR leaders reading those posts are already convinced. They don't need to be persuaded that wellness is important. They need help answering a different set of questions:

- Will this program actually get adopted, or will it collect dust?
- How do I present ROI to my CFO?
- What does a realistic rollout look like for a 500-person distributed team?

That's the BOFU gap. And most wellness vendors aren't filling it.

A 3-part test to diagnose where your wellness content is stuck:

**Awareness:** Does this post explain why wellness matters?
**Consideration:** Does this post help an HR leader compare and evaluate options?
**Decision:** Does this post give a buyer the language to defend the purchase internally?

If 80% of your content passes only the awareness test, you're publishing for traffic — not for pipeline.

If you lead wellness content at your org: which layer is your biggest gap?
