# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-17

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The bottom-of-funnel gap most wellness content programs miss — and a 3-part framework to close it

---

Most wellness content teams have a top-of-funnel problem disguised as a content problem.

Your blog gets traffic. Your newsletter gets opens. But nobody's booking demos or downloading the ROI calculator.

That's not a content volume issue. It's a funnel architecture issue.

Here's what's missing in almost every wellness content program I audit:

**Bottom-of-funnel content.**

Specifically:
— Comparison content (your platform vs. the status quo)
— Use case content (what does this actually look like for a 500-person manufacturing company?)
— Decision-stage content (what should a CHRO look for when evaluating a wellness vendor?)

HR decision-makers don't buy from top-of-funnel content. They buy after they've read six articles, compared three vendors, and found the one resource that spoke directly to their situation.

Most wellness brands write for awareness because it's easier to measure clicks than conversations.

If your wellness content is pulling traffic but not pipeline — ask yourself: when did you last publish something for the person who is 80% decided?

That gap is where deals are lost.
