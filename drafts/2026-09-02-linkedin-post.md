# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-02

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU blindspot: why wellness content teams fill the top of the funnel but miss decision-stage buyers entirely

---

Most wellness content teams don't have a strategy problem. They have a BOFU blindspot.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

The content calendar is full. Burnout prevention guides, mental health tip roundups, stress management listicles. Traffic numbers look decent. But demo requests? Flat.

The problem: everything you've published is awareness content. It attracts people who want to learn — not people who want to buy.

HR leaders who are 30 days from signing a wellness platform aren't Googling "what is employee wellbeing." They're searching for:
— ROI data on wellness programs
— How to make the business case to the CFO
— What a wellness implementation looks like in a manufacturing plant

That content barely exists in most vendors' libraries.

A 3-part filter before commissioning any new piece:
1. Could someone read this without ever considering a purchase? (If yes, it's TOFU.)
2. Does this help an HR leader justify a decision — to themselves or to leadership?
3. Is there a logical CTA path here?

If you can't pass #2, the piece isn't strategic. It's just content.

The gap isn't awareness. It's decision-stage content that actually moves buyers.

What does your current content ratio look like — TOFU vs. BOFU?
