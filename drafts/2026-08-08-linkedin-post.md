**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — why HR vendors win on traffic and lose on demos

---

Most corporate wellness content clusters are built almost entirely for awareness.

TOFU performs well: "benefits of employee wellness," "how to reduce burnout," "what is an EAP." These rank. They get traffic. HR professionals share them.

But then what?

The gap is BOFU — decision-stage content. The stuff an HR leader needs when they're already convinced wellness matters and now have to make a business case to their CFO.

That content looks like:
→ ROI calculators and cost-of-burnout frameworks
→ Implementation timelines ("what does rollout actually look like")
→ Measurement guides — how do I prove this program is working?
→ Comparison frameworks HR leaders share with leadership before sign-off

Most wellness vendors skip this because it feels "too salesy." But HR leaders aren't passive readers. They're building internal cases. They need your numbers, your specificity, your proof.

If your wellness content is getting strong organic traffic but weak demo conversion, this is the gap.

Build the bottom of the funnel before you build more top.

What does your most-used decision-stage content look like right now?
