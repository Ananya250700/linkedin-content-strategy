# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-25

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Wellness blogs that rank but don't convert reveal an intent mismatch problem, not an SEO problem — and most wellness content teams never fix it because they're measuring the wrong metric.

---

Your wellness blog is ranking on page one. And nobody is requesting a demo.

That's not an SEO problem. It's an intent mismatch problem — and most B2B wellness brands never fix it because they're measuring the wrong thing.

Here's what's usually happening: the blog is optimized for informational keywords — "what is employee wellness," "benefits of wellness programs." Great for traffic. Zero buying intent.

The HR leader reading that post isn't evaluating a solution. They're looking for a definition. You gave them one. They bounced.

What converts isn't the informational post — it's the content that meets HR leaders mid-decision: when they're already sold on wellness but trying to justify budget, choose a vendor, or build the business case internally.

That content looks different:
— Vendor comparison posts
— Business case templates
— ROI benchmarks by company size
— "What to ask a wellness vendor" guides

Most wellness content teams know this gap exists. They keep publishing top-of-funnel anyway because it's easier to justify with traffic numbers.

How is your team measuring wellness content success — traffic or pipeline influence?
