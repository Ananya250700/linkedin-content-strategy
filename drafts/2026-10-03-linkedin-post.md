**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is worth writing about — challenges the instinct to pick topics by gut feel or leadership preference

---

Most wellness content teams pick topics based on what feels timely or what leadership wants to publish.

That's how you end up with 12 blogs about "work-life balance" that nobody reads twice.

Here's the filter I use before greenlighting any wellness topic:

**1. Is there a real decision to make?**
If an HR leader reads this and has nothing to decide, act on, or implement — skip it. Content that doesn't move someone toward a decision is just noise.

**2. Does it answer a question they're already asking?**
Not a question you think they should have. One they're actually typing into Google or bringing up in HR roundtables. Topic ideation without search validation is guesswork dressed up as strategy.

**3. Can you say something the vendor website hasn't?**
If your angle matches three competitor blogs already ranking, you're not adding signal — you're adding volume. Volume doesn't build authority.

Run any topic through this before you brief it. You'll kill half your backlog.

And the half that remains will actually do something.
