# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-05

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most teams build for awareness, not decision-making

---

Most corporate wellness content is built for awareness.

"5 signs of employee burnout." "Why mental health at work matters." "The business case for wellness programs."

HR leaders already know burnout is real. They're not reading your blog to learn that.

They're reading it to figure out: how do I actually fix this? What does implementation look like? How do I justify this spend to my CFO?

That's the BOFU gap — and it's where almost every wellness content team stalls.

The content that drives demo requests isn't content that explains the problem. It's content that makes the decision easier.

Three questions that fill the gap:
1. What does your buyer need to know right before they request a demo?
2. What internal objections will they face — and can content address those proactively?
3. What would make a skeptical CFO say yes?

If you can't answer those, your wellness blog is building an audience that never converts.

Most wellness content is written for employees. The decision-maker is someone else entirely.

What's the most useful piece of content a wellness vendor ever gave you before you signed?
