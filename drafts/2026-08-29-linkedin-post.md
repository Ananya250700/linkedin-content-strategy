# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-29

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU funnel gap most wellness content teams ignore — TOFU content is abundant; decision-stage content that helps HR leaders make the internal case is nearly nonexistent.

---

Most wellness content strategies are built entirely for awareness.

Blog posts about burnout. Thought leadership on mental health at work. Guides on work-life balance. All TOFU. All necessary. None of it closes.

Here's the funnel gap I see constantly in B2B wellness content:

No BOFU content exists. At all.

When an HR leader is 80% convinced they need a wellness platform, what do they find? Competitor comparisons on software review sites. Case studies buried in PDFs no one downloads. A demo page with stock photos.

That's the real gap — not awareness content (there's plenty of that). The gap is decision-stage content that helps an HR leader make the internal case.

The 3 pieces most wellness content teams are missing:

**1. ROI frameworks** — not a whitepaper. A real decision tool an HR leader can use in a budget conversation.

**2. Implementation reality content** — what rollout actually looks like, including the friction. HR leaders trust vendors who acknowledge the hard parts.

**3. Internal pitch content** — something they can take into a leadership meeting. A one-pager. A deck skeleton. A talking-points doc.

TOFU drives sessions. BOFU drives sign-offs.

If your wellness content program isn't generating pipeline, audit your funnel before you write another awareness piece.
