# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-06

**Pillar:** Pillar 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** The counterintuitive audit: why I pulled a 4,000-visit blog post from our optimization roadmap (and what the real criteria are)

---

High traffic is not the same as the right traffic.

Last quarter, I audited a blog post on our wellness platform pulling 4,000+ monthly visits. Most content teams would call that a win.

I pulled it from our optimization queue.

Here's what I look at before I touch any post — and traffic is the last item on the list:

**1. Who's actually reading it?**
That post was attracting job seekers and students, not HR managers evaluating wellness vendors. Great numbers, wrong audience entirely.

**2. What's the conversion path?**
Zero CTAs matched their intent. No demo clicks. No newsletter signups from that segment. Just bounce.

**3. Is the topic fixable — or fundamentally wrong?**
You can reframe a post to change the audience signal. But if the topic doesn't sit in an HR buyer's decision journey, a rewrite won't save it.

The post stayed live — it wasn't actively hurting us. But it came off the roadmap.

For B2B wellness content, traffic without buyer intent is noise you're actively choosing to optimize.

What metric is your content team actually chasing?
