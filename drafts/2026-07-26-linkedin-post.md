# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-07-26

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss — TOFU is full, BOFU is empty

---

Most wellness content teams are solving the wrong problem.

They're building awareness content — "what is employee wellbeing," "10 benefits of a wellness program" — and wondering why none of it generates demo requests.

The issue isn't content quality. It's funnel positioning.

Here's the gap I see repeatedly: **TOFU is full. BOFU is empty.**

Awareness-stage content is the easiest to commission and the hardest to convert from. A VP of HR reading "why wellness matters" already knows why wellness matters. They're not searching for a reason to buy — they're searching for a reason to choose.

The 3 content types that actually move BOFU for HR tech:

**1. Implementation content** — "How to launch a wellness program in a 500-person manufacturing company." Specific, operational, reduces perceived risk.

**2. Comparison content** — Honest side-by-sides that respect the buyer's intelligence, not feature dumps dressed as guides.

**3. ROI evidence** — Not generic industry stats, but outcome frameworks tied to their actual cost centers (absenteeism, attrition, productivity loss).

Before you brief your next wellness blog: ask where in the funnel the person reading it actually is.

If the answer is "anyone curious about wellness" — you're writing for reach, not for revenue.

Which of these three is missing from your current content mix?
