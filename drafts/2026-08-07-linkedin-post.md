# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-07

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Your wellness blog is ranking but not converting — because most B2B wellness content is built for the wrong buyer stage.

---

Your wellness content is ranking. It's not converting. Those aren't the same problem.

High traffic on a wellness blog means you solved an SEO problem. A quiet demo pipeline means you haven't solved a buyer problem.

Most B2B wellness platforms write content that serves the curious employee — "how to prevent burnout," "workplace stress statistics," "wellness program ideas." It performs in search. But the person searching those terms isn't the person signing your contract.

The CHRO evaluating a wellness platform isn't Googling "how to reduce stress at work." They're looking for ROI frameworks, vendor comparisons, and implementation guides — content that's harder to produce and lives at the bottom of the funnel.

Before I write any wellness piece now, I ask one question: which stage of the buyer journey does this serve?

TOFU gets traffic. BOFU drives pipeline. Most wellness content teams end up 80% TOFU by default — because it's easier to rank and feels safer to publish.

If your wellness blog is full but your funnel is quiet, that's the gap worth fixing first.

What's stopping your team from building more BOFU content?
