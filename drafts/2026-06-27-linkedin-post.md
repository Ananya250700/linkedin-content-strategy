# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-06-27

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most brands build strong awareness layers but produce nothing that helps HR leaders compare, justify, or decide.

---

Most B2B wellness platforms have the same content problem: thousands of monthly readers, single-digit demo conversion.

The gap isn't the content quality. It's the funnel.

Wellness content teams build strong awareness layers — "what is employee burnout," "10 signs of workplace stress" — and then nothing. No content that helps an HR leader make the case internally, compare platforms, or actually decide.

That's the BOFU gap. And almost every wellness vendor ignores it.

BOFU wellness content doesn't look like "Book a Demo." It looks like:
— "What to include in your wellness program RFP"
— "How to calculate ROI on an EAP investment"
— "Wellness platform vs. EAP: which one does your team actually need"

These posts don't drive traffic. They drive buyers.

If your wellness blog ranks #1 for "employee wellbeing strategies" but your sales team is still qualifying cold leads — your content is doing awareness work, not sales support.

Fix the funnel before you fix the traffic.

What does your wellness content look like below the awareness layer?
