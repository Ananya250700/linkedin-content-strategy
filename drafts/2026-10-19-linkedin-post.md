# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-19

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Traffic without conversion is a content strategy failure, not a sales failure — wellness content is optimized to rank, not to convert HR decision-makers.

---

Your wellness blog is getting traffic. Your demo pipeline isn't moving.

That's not a sales problem. That's a content problem.

Most corporate wellness content is built to rank — not to help. You optimize for "best employee wellness programs" and end up with a 1,500-word list that tells HR leaders nothing they didn't already know.

The content performs on paper. Impressions up. Rankings improving. Conversions: flat.

Here's what's actually happening: you're answering search queries, not solving the real problems HR leaders bring to vendor conversations.

A CHRO doesn't search "wellness program ideas." They're thinking:
— How do I justify this budget to the CFO?
— What does real adoption look like at scale?
— Why did our last initiative fail quietly?

Wellness content that converts addresses those questions directly. With specifics. With data. With an honest take on what most programs get wrong.

Traffic without conversion is a signal your content is too safe.

If your content doesn't make a CHRO uncomfortable at least once — it's probably not doing enough work.

What's the last piece of wellness content that genuinely changed how you think about something?
