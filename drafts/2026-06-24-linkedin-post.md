**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is worth writing about — because most teams pick topics the wrong way

---

Most wellness content teams pick topics the wrong way.

They look at search volume. They check what competitors are writing. They brainstorm what feels relevant this quarter.

None of that tells you whether the topic will actually help HR leaders make a decision.

At Vantage Fit, I use a 3-part filter before any wellness topic makes it to a brief:

**1. Does it map to a buying trigger?**
Not just "HR leaders care about this" — is this a topic they're actively wrestling with right now? Budget season, post-survey action planning, and Q4 wellness resets are triggers. "Employee engagement" alone is not.

**2. Does it have an uncomfortable sub-question?**
If the topic is safe and easy to write, it's probably already been written a hundred times. The version worth publishing names the part most wellness vendors skip.

**3. Does it answer "so what do I do Monday morning?"**
HR leaders don't need more research. They need something actionable enough to start.

Traffic doesn't validate a topic. Conversion does.

What's the last wellness piece you cut because it didn't pass a test like this?
