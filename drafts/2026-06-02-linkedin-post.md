**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** What I actually check before optimizing a blog post — intent alignment before keyword gap analysis

---

Most content teams optimize for rankings. I optimize for relevance decay.

When I audit a blog for Vantage Fit, the first thing I check isn't keyword position. It's whether the post still answers the question HR leaders are actually asking in 2026 — not 2022 when it was written.

A post ranking #4 for "employee wellness program ideas" might pull 2,000 visits a month. But if the content was written for HR generalists running a perks program, and your actual buyer is now a CHRO evaluating ROI on wellness spend — that traffic is noise, not pipeline.

My pre-optimization checklist, in order:

1. Who is this post written for? Does that still match our ICP?
2. What decision does it help the reader make? Is that decision still relevant?
3. Is it driving any downstream action — demo, resource download, related blog? Or is it a dead end?

Keyword gap analysis comes fourth. After intent alignment.

The posts that hurt most to kill are the high-traffic ones. But traffic that doesn't convert is just vanity in a spreadsheet.
