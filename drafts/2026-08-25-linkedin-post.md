# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-25

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** Traffic intent and buyer intent are not the same keyword cluster — and most wellness content teams haven't mapped the difference.

---

Most B2B wellness blogs are optimized for Google, not for the person making a buying decision.

I see this pattern constantly: high-traffic posts ranking for "employee wellness tips" or "workplace mental health statistics" — with zero conversion downstream. The traffic looks good. The demo pipeline doesn't move.

Here's what I've learned doing this at Vantage Fit:

Traffic intent and buyer intent are not the same keyword cluster.

A post ranking for "why is employee wellness important" is attracting HR generalists doing research. A post ranking for "how to build a wellness program for manufacturing employees" is finding an HR leader with a live problem and a budget to solve it.

The second post gets a tenth of the traffic. But it converts.

When I audit content now, I split every piece into one of three categories:
— Awareness: for reach, not pipeline
— Consideration: for leads who are evaluating
— Decision: for buyers who need permission to sign off

Most wellness content teams are over-indexed on awareness. The BOFU is almost always empty.

If your traffic isn't converting — before you look at the writing, look at what you're writing about.

Is your content cluster actually structured around a buying decision?
