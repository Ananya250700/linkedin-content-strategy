# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-25

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU funnel gap — why wellness content teams generate traffic but not pipeline

---

Most wellness content strategies have a TOFU problem disguised as a content problem.

The content calendar is full. Blog cadence is consistent. Traffic is growing. But demo requests are flat.

Here's why: the content funnel for wellness platforms is almost entirely educational. You're publishing "what is employee burnout" and "10 signs of workplace stress" — and those posts rank. But they're attracting researchers, not buyers.

BOFU wellness content looks completely different:
— "How to choose between employee wellness vendors" (comparison intent)
— "What a wellness program ROI calculation actually looks like" (justification intent)
— "What HR leaders should ask in a wellness platform demo" (purchase intent)

These aren't the posts that win awards or get reshared in HR Slack groups. They're the posts that convert.

The gap isn't a keyword problem. It's a strategy problem — most wellness content teams optimize for reach, not revenue.

Before you add another awareness post to your calendar: does your bottom-of-funnel content exist? Does it answer the question an HR decision-maker is asking right before they buy?

That's the audit worth running.
