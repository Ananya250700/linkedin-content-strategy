**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** I reversed the order most B2B content teams use — start with the buyer decision, then find the keyword, not the other way around.

---

Most B2B content teams start with keyword research. I reversed the order.

Not because keywords don't matter — they do. But when search volume drives the content calendar, you end up building content for crawlers and calling it a strategy.

Here's the actual process I use at Vantage Fit:

Start with the decision, not the search term.

What is the HR leader trying to decide right now — whether to budget for a wellness platform, which vendor to shortlist, how to justify the spend to a skeptical CFO?

Once I know the decision, I map the question they're asking on the way to making it. That question becomes the content brief. Keyword research comes last: finding the phrase that maps to the question, not designing the piece around a phrase.

The difference shows up in how the content reads. Built from a buyer's decision, it sounds like a trusted advisor. Built from keyword volume, it sounds assembled to rank.

HR leaders can tell the difference. Most have stopped reading vendor blogs because of it.

What does your content planning process actually start with?
