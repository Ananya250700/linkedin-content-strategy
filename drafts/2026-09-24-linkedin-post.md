**Pillar:** 4 — Vantage Fit Work Amplification
**Angle:** The engagement cliff in corporate wellness programs — why participation drops 40–60% by Month 3, and what the data shows actually fixes it.

---

Most corporate wellness programs see strong early participation. Then Month 3 arrives.

We've tracked this pattern repeatedly across wellness program data: participation peaks in Weeks 2–3 post-launch, then drops 40–60% before the quarter ends.

HR teams usually diagnose this as a communication problem. More emails. Better reminders. A new challenge to re-spark interest.

But the drop isn't about awareness. It's about relevance.

The employees who stay engaged past Month 3 share one trait: they connected the program to something personally specific — a health goal, a chronic condition, a performance concern. The ones who churned never made that connection.

This is the design gap most wellness programs miss. They're built for broad launch appeal, not sustained personal relevance.

At Vantage Fit, we call it the engagement cliff. It's predictable. And it's fixable — but only if you design for individual relevance from the start, not just program awareness.

The fix isn't more reminders. It's better onboarding — personalized pathways that help employees connect the platform to something they actually care about in Week 1.

What does your wellness program's onboarding experience look like for a new employee? That's where retention is won or lost.
