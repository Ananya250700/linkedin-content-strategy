# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-17

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Why your wellness blog drives traffic but not demo requests — the gap between awareness content and content that actually moves HR leaders toward action

---

Your wellness blog is getting traffic. It's probably not getting you leads.

Most corporate wellness content is optimized for search, not for decision-making. You rank for "employee burnout signs" or "workplace stress management" — but the HR leader reading it doesn't know what to do next. They finish the article and close the tab.

The gap isn't the content quality. It's the architecture.

Wellness content that converts does three things that most awareness content skips:

1. It addresses a problem the HR leader is already trying to solve internally — not just educating them that the problem exists
2. It gives a concrete next step: a framework, a diagnostic, a question to take into their next leadership meeting
3. It acknowledges the real barriers — budget, leadership buy-in, employee skepticism — instead of presenting wellness as a frictionless 3-step rollout

Traffic tells you what people are curious about. Inbound tells you what they're desperate to fix.

If your wellness content funnel ends at the blog, you're building an audience. Not a pipeline.
