# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-03

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** What I actually check before optimizing a blog (it's not keywords first)

---

Most content teams optimize the wrong thing first.

They pull a blog with 5,000 monthly visitors, see a ranking opportunity, and start rewriting for keywords. Six weeks later — same position, same conversion rate.

Here's what I check before I touch a single word:

**1. Who is actually landing here?**
If a piece ranking for "employee wellness tips" is pulling HR coordinators instead of CHROs, keyword work won't move demo requests. Audience-intent mismatch is the real problem.

**2. Is the CTA earning its place?**
Most wellness blogs end with a generic "learn more." That's not a CTA — it's a shrug. I look at what action makes sense for the reader at that stage, then build the close around it.

**3. Is the post ranking for a keyword it has no business owning?**
Sometimes it is. Patching the content doesn't fix that. Retiring or redirecting is often the smarter call.

Traffic is a vanity metric if it's pulling the wrong people at the wrong stage.
