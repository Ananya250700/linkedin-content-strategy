# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-17

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Most B2B wellness content is built to rank, not to convert — and that gap is costing HR tech brands pipeline.

---

Most wellness content ranks. Almost none of it converts.

The traffic is real. The demo pipeline? Quiet.

Here's why: wellness content teams optimize for search intent, not buyer intent. They chase "workplace burnout statistics" and "employee wellness program examples" because those terms get volume. They do. And they pull in HR managers doing research — not CHROs ready to evaluate a platform.

The fix isn't more content. It's a different question before you write:

Is this topic on the path to a buying decision?

Wellness content sits in three places:
— Awareness: educates, builds trust
— Consideration: helps HR compare approaches
— Decision: gives a CHRO what they need to make the case internally

Most B2B wellness blogs live in awareness. Almost nothing touches decision. That's the gap where pipeline dies.

Before I write any wellness piece now, I ask: which HR leader reads this, and what do they do next? If I can't answer that, the topic goes back to the queue.

Traffic without intent is vanity dressed as strategy.

What does your wellness content actually move — awareness or pipeline?
