# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-12

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Wellness content drives traffic but not pipeline because it's written to close, not to inform

---

Most wellness vendor blogs rank well and convert almost nothing.

The reason isn't SEO. It's intent mismatch.

HR professionals visit wellness content to build a business case they've already started forming — or to understand a problem before they're ready to act. They're in research mode. They're not ready to book a demo.

But most wellness content is written to close. It leads with product features, buries the actual insight, and ends with a CTA that assumes the reader is already sold.

The fix isn't more CTAs. It's mapping content to where the reader actually is.

Here's how I think about it at Vantage Fit:

**Top of funnel:** Name the problem honestly. "Why wellness programs fail" consistently outperforms "5 wellness ideas" — because HR leaders already know programs fail. They want to understand why.

**Middle of funnel:** Give them the framework to evaluate solutions, including yours. If your content can't survive comparison, your product probably can't either.

**Bottom of funnel:** Specificity over testimonials. An HR leader wants to know what happened in their industry — not a generic success story with no context.

Traffic is a vanity metric until you know where readers are exiting the funnel.
