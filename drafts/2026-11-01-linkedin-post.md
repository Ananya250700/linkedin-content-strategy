# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-11-01

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders  
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most wellness content teams invest heavily in awareness, but nothing exists for the final 10% of the buying journey when CHROs are actually making renewal or procurement decisions.

---

Most workplace wellness content stops at awareness.

Blogs on "5 signs of burnout." Infographics on "why sleep matters." All useful. None of it moves an HR leader closer to a decision.

The BOFU gap in wellness content is the most overlooked problem in HR tech content strategy. Here's what it looks like: your top-of-funnel pieces get traffic. Your mid-funnel guides get downloads. But when a CHRO sits in a budget meeting asking "why should we renew this wellness platform?" — there's nothing written for that moment.

Bottom-of-funnel wellness content doesn't look like a blog. It looks like:  
→ ROI calculators with actual workforce data  
→ Case studies that name the business problem, not just the program  
→ Comparison guides written for procurement, not HR generalists  

The companies winning wellness platform deals aren't the ones with the best employer brand blog. They're the ones whose content does the work in the final 10% of the buying journey.

If you're building wellness content right now: what's in your BOFU? A renewal justification deck doesn't count.
