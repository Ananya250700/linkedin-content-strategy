# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-30

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Wellness content that ranks well still fails to generate pipeline because it's written for the algorithm, not the decision-maker

---

A wellness blog with 10,000 monthly visitors and zero demo requests isn't an SEO problem. It's a positioning problem.

Most B2B wellness content is written for the algorithm, not the CHRO.

The posts rank. The keywords are researched. The structure is clean. But the content assumes the reader doesn't know what employee burnout is — when in reality, the HR leader reading it has been trying to solve burnout for two years and is looking for a reason to act.

There's a gap between awareness-stage content and decision-stage content. Most wellness brands live entirely in awareness.

Here's what converting content does differently: it names the specific problem the reader is already feeling (not explaining it), challenges an assumption they're still holding, and gives them language to take back to their CFO.

Traffic tells you people found you. Conversion tells you they trusted you enough to move.

Before you optimize your next wellness post for search, ask: Would a CHRO who's already sold on wellness find anything here worth acting on?

That's the question most wellness content teams skip.
