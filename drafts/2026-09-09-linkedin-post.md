# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-09

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss — BOFU barely exists in most HR content libraries, and that's where conversion is dying.

---

Most wellness content teams don't have a BOFU problem. They have a BOFU absence problem.

When I audit content clusters for HR platforms, the gap is almost always the same: nothing for the HR leader who is already sold on wellness and now needs to justify the budget, compare vendors, or build an implementation plan.

That's BOFU. And it's where most wellness content stops.

A framework I use when planning a content quarter:

→ **TOFU:** Education. Trend-driven. "Why this matters."
→ **MOFU:** Evaluation. Comparison. "What good looks like."
→ **BOFU:** Decision. Validation. "How to make this work at your company."

Most wellness content lives at TOFU. Some reaches MOFU. BOFU is almost always an afterthought — one "request a demo" page carrying all the weight.

HR leaders don't convert on traffic. They convert when a piece of content answers the exact question they have during vendor evaluation.

If your BOFU library is thin, that's where your conversion drop-off is coming from.

What does your BOFU content actually look like right now?
