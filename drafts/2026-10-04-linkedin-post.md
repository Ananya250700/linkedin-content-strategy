# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-04

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is actually worth writing about

---

Most wellness content teams ask the wrong question.

They ask: "What should we write about next?"

The better question is: "Is this actually worth writing about?"

One generates a content calendar. The other generates content that earns trust with HR leaders — and moves them closer to a decision.

Before I add any topic to Vantage Fit's content queue, I run it through three filters:

**1. Is there a real decision at the end of this?**
If an HR leader finishes reading and has nothing to act on, it's informational — not useful. Useful content closes a gap they're already aware of.

**2. Does it name something uncomfortable?**
The pieces that get shared internally are the ones that acknowledge a genuine problem — burnout data that's hard to ignore, program results that show disengagement hasn't moved.

**3. Do we have a credible perspective here?**
Generic takes drive traffic. Perspectives built on real patterns from HR teams drive trust. There's a difference.

Topics that fail all three: polished thought leadership nobody needs.
Topics that pass all three: the posts that actually land in CHRO inboxes.

What's your filter before something makes it into the queue?
