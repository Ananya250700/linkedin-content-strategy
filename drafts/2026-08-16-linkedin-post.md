# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-16

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss (BOFU) — why wellness brands flood the top of the funnel and go silent exactly when HR leaders are ready to decide.

---

Most corporate wellness content is built for awareness. Almost none of it is built for decisions.

Walk through any B2B wellness vendor's blog. You'll find "10 benefits of employee wellness programs" and "why mental health at work matters." Useful for someone just discovering the space. Useless for an HR leader who already believes in wellness and now needs to choose a solution, justify budget, or convince their CHRO.

That gap — between awareness and decision — is where most wellness content goes quiet.

Here's how I think about the funnel for HR-facing content:

**TOFU:** "Why does this matter?" → Most brands have this in excess.
**MOFU:** "How do I approach this?" → Some brands have this.
**BOFU:** "How do I decide / justify this?" → Almost nobody builds this.

BOFU content for HR looks like: ROI calculators, implementation case studies, comparison guides, budget justification frameworks. Not traffic drivers — trust builders.

If your content team is reporting on pageviews but not on demo requests or MQL quality, you're optimizing the wrong thing.

What's the last piece of BOFU content your wellness vendor (or content team) published?
