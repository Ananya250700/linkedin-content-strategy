# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-05-31

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — why wellness content drives traffic but not demos, and what decision-stage content actually looks like

---

Most wellness content teams have the same problem: too much awareness content, not enough decision content.

You've got blogs on "what is employee wellness" and "benefits of a wellness program." You've got downloadable guides and infographics.

What you don't have: content that helps an HR leader make the actual decision to buy or build.

That's the BOFU gap. And it costs wellness brands more demos than bad SEO ever will.

Here's what decision-stage wellness content actually looks like:

**Comparison content.** Not "wellness program vs. no wellness program." Specific: "Wellness app vs. internal wellbeing committee — what works at what headcount."

**Implementation content.** Not case studies. Step-by-step: "How to launch a wellness program in a manufacturing unit with no dedicated HR budget."

**Objection content.** The questions your sales team hears every week — answered publicly, in writing, before the demo.

Most wellness vendors avoid this content because it feels too close to a sales pitch. That's exactly why it works.

If your wellness content is driving traffic but not pipeline, you're probably filling the top of the funnel while leaving the bottom empty.
