# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-19

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams over-invest in awareness and leave decision-stage buyers without what they need to say yes.

---

Most corporate wellness content is stuck at the top of the funnel.

You've got blog posts on "why employee wellbeing matters." Infographics on burnout statistics. Awareness content that performs well in search and dies quietly at the demo form.

The problem isn't the content. It's the gap.

HR leaders actively evaluating wellness platforms don't need to be convinced wellness matters — they already know. What they need at the bottom of the funnel:
— Implementation guides (what does rollout actually look like?)
— ROI benchmarks grounded in real company data
— Comparison content that treats them like decision-makers, not prospects

Most wellness content teams optimize for traffic. BOFU content optimizes for trust at the moment of purchase.

The shift isn't complicated. Before briefing your next piece, ask one question: is this for someone who just discovered the problem, or someone already sold on the solution who needs to justify the budget?

That question alone eliminates half the content that never converts.

What does the bottom of your wellness content funnel look like right now?
