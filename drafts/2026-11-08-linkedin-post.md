**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is worth writing about — before a word gets written.

---

Most wellness content teams are creating topics, not solving problems. There's a difference.

Here's the 3-part filter I use before we write anything at Vantage Fit:

**1. Does an HR leader have to explain this to their leadership?**
If yes, they need content that makes the business case — not feel-good tips. Business case content converts. Feel-good content gets bookmarked and forgotten.

**2. Does it get searched with intent, not just curiosity?**
High traffic ≠ high value. I want HR decision-makers looking for a solution, not a definition. Informational search volume is a vanity metric if your goal is pipeline.

**3. Does it move someone from "aware" to "ready to act"?**
If a topic only informs, it belongs in a glossary. If it changes what an HR leader does next week, it belongs in the content plan.

Most wellness content teams optimize for coverage. That's how you end up with 50 blog posts and no demo requests.

The filter isn't about restricting creativity — it's about making sure every piece does actual work.
