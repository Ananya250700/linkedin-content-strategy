# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-05-30

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most B2B wellness content teams publish almost exclusively awareness content, leaving the bottom of the funnel empty and sales conversations doing the job content should have already done.

---

Most B2B wellness content teams have the same invisible problem: 80% of what they publish is awareness content.

They write about why wellness matters, why burnout is rising, why HR needs to act. Strong traffic. Zero pipeline.

The gap is BOFU — bottom-of-funnel content that talks to HR leaders already convinced of the case for wellness. Leaders now trying to justify a budget, choose a platform, or build an internal rollout plan.

Here's the ratio I look for when auditing a wellness content library:

**Top-of-funnel:** Why wellness matters → educates, attracts traffic
**Middle-of-funnel:** How wellness programs work → builds consideration
**Bottom-of-funnel:** How to prove ROI, build the business case, implement → converts

Most teams think they're at 7:2:1. The honest audit puts them closer to 9:1:0.

If your sales team is fielding CFO objections in demos that your content should have already answered — you have a BOFU gap, not a sales problem.

What's the last piece of BOFU content your wellness brand published?
