# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-21

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — why wellness content generates traffic but not pipeline, and the 3-piece fix

---

Most corporate wellness content is designed for awareness. That's exactly why it doesn't convert.

HR leaders don't need more articles explaining what burnout is. They've lived it. What they actually need is content that helps them decide — which programs are worth funding, how to calculate ROI, how to pitch leadership on a wellness budget.

That's BOFU content. And almost no wellness vendor publishes it.

Here's the gap I see in most wellness content strategies:

**TOFU:** "5 signs of employee burnout" — everyone has ten versions of this
**MOFU:** "How to build a wellness program" — decent coverage
**BOFU:** "How to evaluate wellness platforms" / "ROI framework for wellness spend" — near-zero

If your content team produces 80% TOFU and nothing at the bottom, you're building brand awareness for a competitor's sales team. Someone else closes the deal with the ROI calculator your team never built.

The fix isn't complicated: map your top 3 buyer objections (cost, adoption, leadership buy-in) and write one piece of content that directly addresses each. That's your BOFU foundation.

What does the most useful wellness content you've read actually help you *do* — not just understand?
