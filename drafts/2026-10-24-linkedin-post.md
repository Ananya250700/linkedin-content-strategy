# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-24

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU funnel gap most wellness content teams don't know they have

---

Most wellness content teams have a BOFU problem they don't know about.

They're publishing wellness tips, engagement surveys, and awareness campaigns — all top-of-funnel. But when an HR leader is actually ready to invest in a platform, there's nothing to hand them.

BOFU wellness content looks different:
→ Case studies with ROI numbers, not just participation metrics
→ Implementation guides that answer "how do we actually roll this out?"
→ Comparison content that helps HR leaders justify the spend internally

The gap isn't effort. It's intent mapping.

Most wellness content is written to educate. Not to help someone decide. Those are two different jobs — and conflating them is why high-traffic wellness blogs produce almost no demo requests.

A fast audit: look at your last 10 published pieces. How many speak to someone who's already convinced about wellness and now needs to choose a platform or defend the budget?

If the answer is zero or one, you have a funnel gap — and your traffic numbers are hiding it.

What makes BOFU wellness content hard to write? HR leaders rarely tell you what actually closes the deal for them. That insight lives in sales calls, not in keyword tools.
