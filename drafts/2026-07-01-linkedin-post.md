# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-01

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU funnel gap most wellness content teams miss — they build awareness libraries with no decision-stage content, leaving HR buyers educated but unconverted.

---

Your wellness content library probably has 30 educational posts and zero decision-stage pieces.

That's not a content gap. It's a funnel problem.

Most HR content teams default to awareness — what burnout looks like, why mental health matters at work, the business case for employee wellbeing. That content earns traffic. It doesn't move buyers.

The gap I see most often: nothing that helps an HR leader take action. No ROI breakdown a CHRO can show a CFO. No implementation case study with real timelines. No comparison framework for evaluating vendors. No business case template an overstretched People team can actually use.

Educating a buyer and converting a buyer are different jobs. TOFU content does the first. BOFU content does the second. Most wellness content teams only build the first.

A quick audit worth running:

Count your awareness posts. Count your decision-enabling posts. If the ratio is worse than 5:1, you're generating interest you're not capturing.

Fix starts with one piece: what does an HR leader need to read before they say yes?

What does your content mix look like right now — heavy on education, or balanced across the funnel?
