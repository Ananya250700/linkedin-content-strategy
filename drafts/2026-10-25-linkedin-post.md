# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-25

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss — why TOFU-heavy content stalls pipeline even when traffic is healthy

---

Most wellness content teams have a top-of-funnel problem disguised as a content problem.

They're publishing at full speed — blog after blog on stress management, burnout prevention, work-life balance. Traffic looks okay. Rankings hold. But demo requests? Flat.

Here's what's missing: bottom-of-funnel content built for HR decision-makers.

TOFU wellness content answers "what is this." BOFU content answers "why should I invest, and how do I justify this to my CFO."

HR leaders aren't just consuming wellness content. They're using it to build a business case internally. That means they need:

— ROI breakdowns, not just outcome statistics
— Implementation guides that address procurement friction
— Comparison content that shows what good looks like vs. what they currently have

Most wellness vendors don't write this content because it's harder. It requires knowing how budget decisions actually get made inside a company — not just what sounds reassuring on a wellness landing page.

If your wellness blog drives traffic but not pipeline, audit your funnel distribution. You probably have 80% awareness content and 20% decision content.

Flip that ratio for your ICP. The traffic won't spike. The conversations will.

What's your current split between awareness and decision-stage content?
