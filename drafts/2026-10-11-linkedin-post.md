# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-10-11

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss — why BOFU content is where buyer decisions actually happen, and why most vendors ignore it

---

Most wellness content teams are building the top of the funnel and calling it a strategy.

HR leaders don't need another article about why employee well-being matters. They already believe it. What they need is content that helps them make the decision — justify budget to the CFO, run a pilot, prove ROI after 90 days.

That's the bottom of the funnel. And it's where most wellness vendors go completely silent.

The gap looks like this:

**Top of funnel:** "Why wellness at work matters in 2026" → high traffic, low intent
**Middle of funnel:** "How to evaluate wellness platforms" → qualified, but general
**Bottom of funnel:** ROI frameworks, implementation playbooks, comparison guides → converts

Most teams have 20 posts at the top and 2 at the bottom — then wonder why content doesn't drive demos.

At Vantage Fit, the content that actually moves buyers is the stuff that answers the question an HR leader has the week before they sign.

If you're evaluating a wellness platform right now — what content actually helped you decide?
