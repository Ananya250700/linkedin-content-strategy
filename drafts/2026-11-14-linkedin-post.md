# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-14

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is actually worth writing — most teams pick topics based on trends or instinct, not buyer stage or intent.

---

Most wellness content teams don't have a topic selection problem. They have a prioritization problem.

They write about burnout because it's trending. They write about mental health because leadership asked for it. They write about work-life balance because a competitor did.

None of that is a strategy.

Before I write any wellness topic for Vantage Fit, it goes through three filters:

**1. Does it match where the HR buyer actually is?**
Early-stage buyers need education. Evaluating buyers need proof. Most wellness content targets awareness — it misses the buyer who's already sold on wellness and needs to make the internal case.

**2. Does it answer what HR leaders search for — not what they post about?**
There's a gap. What people share on LinkedIn and what they're typing at 11pm when they're stuck are very different queries. The second one earns trust.

**3. Does it have a clear next action?**
If the reader walks away informed but not directed, the content served them, not the business.

Most wellness blog briefs skip filters 1 and 3 entirely.

Which filter does your content team consistently miss?
