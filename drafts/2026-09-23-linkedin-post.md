# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-23

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** Most wellness content libraries are 80% awareness-stage — HR leaders in buying mode can't find a single piece that helps them decide or build an internal business case.

---

Here's an uncomfortable truth for HR content leaders at wellness platforms:

Most of your content was built to attract HR professionals. Almost none of it was built to help them decide.

Run this audit on your content library. Sort every piece into one of three stages:

**Stage 1 — Awareness.** Content that explains why wellness matters. Why burnout is expensive. What a good wellness program looks like. Most wellness platforms have 60–80% of their library here.

**Stage 2 — Evaluation.** Content that helps HR leaders compare approaches, understand tradeoffs, or identify what questions to ask a vendor. Rare.

**Stage 3 — Decision.** Content built specifically for the moment an HR leader is trying to get internal approval — ROI frameworks, implementation guides, business case templates, executive-facing one-pagers. Almost nonexistent.

The gap matters because HR leaders don't decide alone. They convince a CFO, a CEO, or a leadership team. If your content doesn't give them the language, data, or frameworks to do that, they'll build the business case without you — and sometimes choose a competitor whose content did the work for them.

Stage 3 content is the hardest to produce and the easiest to deprioritize. It's also the highest-converting.

What percentage of your wellness content library is actually built for the decision stage?
