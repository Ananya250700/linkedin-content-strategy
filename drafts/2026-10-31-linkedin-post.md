# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-31

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams are building a library, not a funnel

---

Most wellness content teams are building a library. Not a funnel.

They publish guides on burnout, stress, sleep, and engagement. Monthly traffic grows. Organic rankings climb. But demo requests don't move.

Here's why: almost all wellness content lives at the top of the funnel. It educates. It never converts.

The gap is BOFU — bottom-of-funnel content. The assets that help an HR leader make a decision, justify budget, or choose between two platforms.

What BOFU wellness content actually looks like:
→ "How to build a business case for a wellness program"
→ "What to ask a wellness vendor before signing"
→ "ROI benchmarks HR leaders use to evaluate wellness spend"

These aren't blog posts. They're decision tools.

If your content strategy doesn't include 2–3 BOFU assets per quarter, you're producing thought leadership that benefits your competitors as much as it benefits you.

The fix is straightforward: audit your content map by funnel stage. If BOFU is empty — that's where next quarter starts.

What does your BOFU look like right now? Curious how other wellness content teams are handling this.
