# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-06-12

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Wellness blogs rank on Google and still fail to move pipeline — because most are written to win search queries, not to answer buyer questions.

---

Your wellness blog is ranking on page one. It's not moving pipeline.

That's not a traffic problem. It's a content strategy problem.

Most B2B wellness content is built to win search queries — "employee burnout statistics," "benefits of workplace wellness programs." These posts rank. They get traffic. They earn backlinks.

They don't convert.

Because ranking answers a search query. Converting answers a buyer question.

"What causes employee burnout" → awareness content.
"How do I build a business case for a wellness program" → decision content.

Most wellness content teams invest heavily in the first. They underinvest in the second — the ROI calculators, implementation guides, comparison pages, and decision frameworks that HR decision-makers actually need before they approve a purchase.

When I audit a wellness content program, I look at one ratio: what percentage of published content targets someone who already knows they have the problem?

Nine times out of ten, it's below 20%.

If your wellness content is ranking and not converting, that's the ratio to fix first.

What does your awareness-to-decision content split look like?
