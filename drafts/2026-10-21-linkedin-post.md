# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-10-21

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams publish only awareness-layer content and miss the moment when HR buyers are actually deciding.

---

Most wellness content teams are publishing for the wrong funnel stage.

Most wellness blogs live in the awareness layer. "What is employee wellness?" "Why does wellbeing matter?" Good for SEO. Useless for closing deals.

Here's the gap nobody talks about: when an HR leader is ready to evaluate a platform, your content should be doing the selling work. But most wellness vendors publish zero BOFU content — nothing that helps a CHRO compare options, build a business case, or justify budget to finance.

The BOFU wellness content most teams are missing:
→ "How to evaluate an employee wellness platform" (written by the vendor, objectively)
→ ROI calculators with cost-per-employee benchmarks
→ Comparison posts that acknowledge competitors honestly
→ Implementation guides that pre-answer the objections finance will ask

This content rarely goes viral. It doesn't drive big traffic numbers. But it sits at the exact moment your buyer is deciding — and that's worth more than 10,000 awareness clicks.

If you're measuring content success by page views, you're optimizing for the wrong metric.

What does your BOFU wellness content look like right now?
