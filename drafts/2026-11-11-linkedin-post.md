**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss: they build strong TOFU but publish almost no BOFU content that actually helps HR leaders decide.

---

Most corporate wellness content teams are building an awareness machine that never converts.

The funnel gap isn't missing content. It's missing the right content at the bottom.

Every wellness platform publishes "10 tips for employee wellbeing" because it ranks. What they rarely publish: content that helps an HR leader actually decide to buy, implement, or propose a wellness program to their CFO.

BOFU wellness content looks different:
— ROI calculators tied to real burnout and absenteeism data
— Implementation guides that answer "how do I get leadership buy-in?"
— Case studies built around measurable outcomes, not generic testimonials
— Content that addresses procurement questions, not just curiosity

The result of skipping this: high traffic, low pipeline. You've educated your audience. You haven't convinced them.

If your wellness blog pulls 20k+ monthly readers but demo requests are flat, check your BOFU coverage before adding more awareness content.
