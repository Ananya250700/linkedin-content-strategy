**Pillar:** Pillar 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** What I check before I optimize a wellness blog — and it's not keywords first

---

Most SEO optimization starts in the wrong place.

Before I touch a single keyword on a wellness blog, I check three things — and keywords aren't one of them.

**1. Does the search intent match what we're actually selling?**
A post ranking for "employee wellness programs" might be pulling informational seekers, not HR decision-makers evaluating platforms. High traffic with low conversion isn't an SEO win — it's a misaligned asset.

**2. Is there a real conversion gap in the content itself?**
We had a blog sitting at #4 on Google for a competitive term. No CTA that spoke to a decision-maker. No comparison angle. No data a CHRO would care about. Ranking meant nothing without a path forward for the reader who was ready to buy.

**3. Can this piece actually be fixed — or should it be retired?**
Not every high-traffic post deserves optimization effort. Some are structurally wrong for the audience. Spending six hours on the wrong piece is worse than killing it and redistributing that effort to something fixable.

Optimization without this audit is just rearranging deck chairs.

What's the first thing you look at before you touch an underperforming blog?
