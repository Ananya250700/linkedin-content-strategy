Manufacturing is the sector most wellness vendors forget.

No standing desks. No "block your calendar" advice. A workforce that's shift-based, physically demanding, and often in environments where talking about mental health still feels like weakness.

I wrote a guide for Vantage Fit on implementing wellness programs in manufacturing — not because it's an easy brief, but because it's genuinely underserved.

What surprised me while researching it: the ROI case for manufacturing wellness is actually cleaner than for office environments. Fewer safety incidents, reduced absenteeism, lower attrition in high-turnover roles. The data exists. The content, mostly, doesn't.

Most wellness content assumes a laptop worker in a hybrid setup. That's maybe 30% of the working population.

The other 70%? They're getting generic tips that don't map to their reality.

If you're an HR leader in manufacturing trying to build the internal case for a wellness initiative — this guide is where I'd start.
