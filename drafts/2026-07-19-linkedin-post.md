# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-19

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — why high-traffic programs don't move pipeline, and the three content types that actually help HR make buying decisions.

---

Most wellness content is built for Google, not for buying decisions.

HR leaders searching "best employee wellness programs" aren't looking for more tips. They're three months into a vendor evaluation, trying to justify a budget to the CFO.

That's bottom-of-funnel intent — and most wellness content teams ignore it entirely.

The pattern I see: brands publish 60 awareness posts for every 1 decision-stage piece. Traffic climbs. Pipeline doesn't move.

The fix isn't more content. It's content built for where buyers actually are:

→ Vendor comparison guides (that actually compare, not just position)
→ ROI calculators with real inputs HR can use in budget conversations
→ Implementation playbooks that help HR teams sell the idea internally

The goal isn't reach. It's to make the decision easier.

If your wellness content is driving 15k monthly readers and zero demo requests, you don't have a traffic problem. You have a funnel architecture problem.

What does your BOFU content look like right now?
