# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-06-26

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Most wellness blogs rank on Google. Almost none of them convert — because they're built for traffic, not for HR buying decisions.

---

Most corporate wellness blogs rank on Google.
Almost none of them convert.

The reason isn't the writing. It's the intent mismatch.

Wellness vendors optimize for traffic — awareness-stage keywords like "benefits of employee wellness" or "why mental health matters at work." HR leaders searching these terms are early in their journey. They're not ready to buy. They might not even know what they're looking for yet.

But the HR leader actually evaluating a wellness platform? They're Googling things like:
— "how to measure ROI of wellness programs"
— "wellness program adoption strategies"
— "how to get leadership buy-in for wellness initiatives"

That content barely exists. Most vendors stop at the top of the funnel and call it thought leadership.

The gap isn't content volume. It's funnel coverage.

If your wellness blog is ranking but not driving qualified pipeline, look at your BOFU content. Not whether you have a buyer's guide — whether you have content that helps an HR leader make the case internally.

That's the content that actually converts.
