**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is worth writing about — challenges the common habit of writing first, validating never.

---

Most wellness content teams pick topics the wrong way.

They start with what feels relevant — stress, burnout, mental health — and write the post. Then wonder why it doesn't convert.

Here's a 3-part filter I use before writing any wellness topic for Vantage Fit:

**1. Is there a decision behind it?**
The best B2B wellness content helps HR leaders make a call — whether to run a program, present a business case, or choose a vendor. If the topic doesn't map to a decision, it's awareness content at best. Fine, but don't mistake it for conversion content.

**2. Does it answer a question they'd actually search at work?**
Not "what is employee wellness" — that's a consumer question. Think: "how to measure ROI of wellness programs" or "wellness benefits that reduce attrition." HR leaders searching at work have budget, authority, and urgency. Match that language.

**3. Can you say something they haven't read 10 times already?**
If you can't add a fresh angle — new data, a counterintuitive take, a real implementation example — don't write it yet. Publish later with something to say, not now with nothing new.

Run any wellness topic through this before you brief a writer or spin up a cluster.
