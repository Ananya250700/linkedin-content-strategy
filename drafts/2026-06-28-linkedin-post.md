# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-06-28

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — why most B2B wellness content teams are overloaded at the top of the funnel and nearly empty at the bottom, losing HR buyers right before the decision

---

Most wellness content teams have a TOFU problem disguised as a content strategy.

They publish 3,000-word guides on "what is employee wellness" and wonder why the blog drives traffic but not demo requests.

The gap isn't content quality. It's funnel depth.

Here's what the average B2B wellness content operation looks like:

TOFU: Overloaded — "how to improve employee wellbeing," "benefits of wellness programs"
MOFU: Thin — a few comparison posts, maybe one case study
BOFU: Nearly empty

The result? You educate HR leaders at scale, then lose them before they ever see what makes your solution different.

BOFU wellness content looks like:
→ "How to evaluate a wellness platform before you buy"
→ "What a 90-day wellness rollout actually looks like"
→ "[Your platform] vs. [competitor]: what CHROs ask us"

This content doesn't rank for massive search volumes. But it's what an HR leader reads right before they book a demo.

If your content pipeline has 10 posts on wellness trends and zero posts on implementation or vendor selection — you're not missing keywords. You're missing the close.

What does the TOFU-to-BOFU ratio look like in your wellness content strategy?
