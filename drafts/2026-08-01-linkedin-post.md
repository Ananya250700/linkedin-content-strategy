The funnel gap killing most corporate wellness content strategies.

Most wellness content teams are working hard and still not converting.

Here's why: 90% of what they publish lives at the top of the funnel. Awareness content. Trend roundups. "Why employee wellness matters" posts that inform but never help anyone decide.

HR professionals already know wellness matters. What they actually need — and almost never find — is content that helps them move forward internally.

That's BOFU content. Bottom of funnel. Decision-stage.

The gap looks like this:

→ TOFU: Plenty — blog posts, stats, trend roundups
→ MOFU: Occasional — guides, frameworks, benchmarks
→ BOFU: Almost nothing — ROI calculators, implementation checklists, vendor comparison content, business case templates

For a CHRO evaluating a wellness platform, the BOFU piece is what gets internal sign-off. Not the "top 10 wellness tips" post.

If your wellness content drives traffic but not demos or inquiries, audit your funnel ratio. The problem is rarely the quality of your writing. It's the distribution of what you're writing.

What does your TOFU-to-BOFU ratio actually look like? My guess: it's closer to 98/2 than 80/20.
