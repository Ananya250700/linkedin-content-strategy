**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The 3-part BOFU filter for wellness content — most content maps die before the decision stage

---

Most wellness content teams have a traffic problem disguised as a strategy problem.

Plenty of blogs. Decent rankings. And almost no content near the moment a buyer actually decides.

That's the BOFU gap — and it's the most common gap I see in HR tech content.

After building content clusters for Vantage Fit, the pattern is consistent: strong awareness layer, weak decision layer. High-traffic posts that never reach the person doing vendor research at 10pm before a budget call.

Here's the 3-part filter I use before committing to any wellness topic:

**1. Decision proximity**
Does this content surface when someone is evaluating options — not just exploring a problem? "How to choose an employee wellness platform" outperforms "Why employee wellness matters" at every stage that actually converts.

**2. HR buyer pain specificity**
Would a CHRO find this useful the week before a board presentation? If it reads like general wellness advice, it won't earn the click that matters.

**3. Proof surface**
Can you embed a case study, a data point, or a real implementation example? No proof = awareness content. You need both — but most teams only build one.

The goal isn't to stop writing top-of-funnel. It's to stop letting your content map end there.

What does your wellness content BOFU actually look like?
