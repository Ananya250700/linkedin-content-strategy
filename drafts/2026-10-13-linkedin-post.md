# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-13

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** Why I retired a high-traffic blog — and what the rewrite actually did to conversions

---

I retired a blog getting 8,000 monthly visits.

Not because it stopped ranking. Because it was the wrong traffic.

The post ranked well for a keyword HR managers search when they're frustrated with their current wellness vendor. High intent signal, right emotional moment — but the content gave them a generic five-step checklist and sent them away satisfied.

Zero demo requests. Zero email signups. Just time-on-page from people we never converted.

Before I decide to optimize, rewrite, or kill a piece, I check three things:

**1. Who is actually landing here?**
Not the keyword — the person behind the search. Same query, different intent depending on where they are in the buying cycle.

**2. What do they do after reading?**
If exit rate is high and the next action isn't ours, the content is a dead end — not an asset.

**3. What's the cost of keeping it alive?**
Outdated, unfocused content erodes topical authority. A post that ranks but doesn't serve anyone is a liability.

I rewrote it with a sharper angle: what wellness vendors get wrong when HR is ready to switch. Added a comparison section. Built the CTA toward the product page, not the newsletter.

Traffic dropped 40%. Conversions tripled.

High traffic is a distraction when it's the wrong audience.
