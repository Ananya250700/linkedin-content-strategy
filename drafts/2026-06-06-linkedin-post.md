# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-06-06

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams are running a traffic operation, not a conversion strategy

---

Most wellness content teams are running a traffic operation disguised as a content strategy.

Fifty blog posts on "signs of employee burnout." Zero content that helps an HR leader justify a wellness platform to their CFO.

That's a BOFU problem. And it's why content-heavy wellness brands still lose deals.

Here's what funnel coverage actually looks like in HR tech content:

**TOFU:** Burnout signs, wellness statistics, trend reports. Brings in traffic. Doesn't close anything.

**MOFU:** Implementation guides, program frameworks, how-to content. Educates. Sometimes converts.

**BOFU:** ROI breakdowns, vendor comparisons, industry-specific case studies, objection-handling content. Rarely exists.

Most teams allocate 80% to TOFU, almost nothing to BOFU — then wonder why demo requests don't follow high-traffic posts.

One question to audit your wellness content stack: Can you point to a piece that helps a CHRO make the internal business case for this purchase?

If you can't, you're writing for Google, not for the buyer.
