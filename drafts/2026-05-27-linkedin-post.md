# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-05-27

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams create awareness content but never build the content that actually converts HR decision-makers

---

Most wellness content teams are producing a lot. And converting very little.

The usual diagnosis: better SEO, more posts, stronger CTAs.

The real problem: nearly all of it is stuck at the top of the funnel.

Here's what I see when I audit wellness content libraries:

→ Dozens of posts on "why employee wellness matters" (awareness)
→ Thought leadership on burnout trends (still awareness)
→ Almost nothing for an HR director who is actively evaluating a solution

BOFU wellness content looks completely different. It's:
- ROI calculators framed for CFO conversations
- Implementation guides for specific industries (manufacturing, remote-first, etc.)
- Comparison content that respects the reader's intelligence
- "How to build the internal business case" — the thing someone searches at 11pm before a leadership meeting

If your wellness content drives traffic but not pipeline, run this check:

Of your last 10 published pieces — how many were written for someone in evaluation mode versus someone vaguely curious?

If the answer skews heavily toward curious, that's your funnel gap. Not your keyword list.

What's one piece of BOFU content you've found actually moves HR buyers?
