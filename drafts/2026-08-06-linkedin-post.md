---

Manufacturing is the most overlooked sector in workplace wellness content.

Most wellness vendors write for knowledge workers — remote employees, desk-based teams, people with flexible schedules. The language, the formats, the metrics. All built for the same audience.

But India has over 12 million manufacturing workers. And the wellness challenges they face — physical hazard exposure, shift fatigue, limited access to mental health support — aren't solved by a step-count challenge or a mindfulness app.

At Vantage Fit, we've been building content for this gap. Not generic content repackaged for a new vertical. A proper implementation guide: what wellness actually looks like for factory floor employees, what metrics HR teams should track, and how to build a program when your workforce is distributed across shifts, not Slack channels.

The insight that shaped it: wellness program adoption in manufacturing isn't a motivation problem. It's a design problem. Programs fail because they're designed for a workforce that doesn't work 9–5, sit at a desk, or check company portals between meetings.

If you're an HR leader in manufacturing, chemicals, or logistics — or a content strategist building for those sectors — I'd like to hear what's actually landing with your teams.
