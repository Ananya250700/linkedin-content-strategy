# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-12

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — why wellness content drives traffic but never converts to pipeline

---

Most wellness content teams are stuck at TOFU. And nobody's saying it out loud.

Here's what it looks like in the data:
- 10,000 monthly visitors to "employee burnout statistics"
- 300 to "how to reduce employee burnout at work"
- 12 to "wellness platform ROI for a 500-person company"

The traffic is real. The buyer intent is invisible — because the content stops before the decision point.

Three questions I ask before writing any wellness blog:

**1. Who is this actually for — someone learning or someone deciding?**
Most wellness content is written for the curious employee, not the CHRO weighing a $50K platform decision. Those aren't the same person.

**2. What does "next" look like for this reader?**
Not every post needs to close a deal. But every post in a cluster should move someone one step forward. If you can't answer what "next" is, the post is floating.

**3. Does this content make a decision easier?**
Traffic isn't trust. The content that converts isn't always the most-read — it's the most useful at the moment of choice.

Wellness content earns traffic by being broadly helpful. It earns demo requests by being specifically useful to someone with budget and a real problem.

What does your BOFU wellness content actually look like?
