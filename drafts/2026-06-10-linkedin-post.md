**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — why wellness content gets traffic but not demo requests, and the 3 content types that actually close it

---

Most wellness content teams have the same gap: brilliant at top-of-funnel, invisible at the bottom.

Here's what that looks like in practice.

A wellness vendor publishes 50 blogs on burnout, engagement, and mental health — all educational, all well-optimized, all targeting awareness keywords. But when an HR leader is three weeks from a vendor decision, none of that content helps them.

BOFU content for wellness isn't about hard selling. It's about answering the questions HR leaders actually ask before signing:

— "What does implementation look like at a company our size?"
— "What do employees actually use?"
— "How do I calculate ROI to justify this to leadership?"

Three content types that close this gap:

1. **ROI benchmarks** — give HR a number they can take upstairs
2. **Implementation walkthroughs** — not polished case studies, actual step-by-step realities
3. **Honest comparison content** — even if it names competitors

Most wellness brands skip this because it feels too sales-y.

That's exactly why it works. Nothing else in the space does it.

If your wellness blog drives traffic but not demo requests, the missing piece is almost always here.
