# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-04

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams build only awareness content, leaving HR decision-makers with nothing to act on when they're ready to buy.

---

Most corporate wellness content lives permanently at the top of the funnel.

Awareness posts. Trend roundups. "Why workplace wellness matters" blogs that everyone clicks and nobody acts on.

The problem isn't the content. The problem is there's nothing waiting for the reader who's already convinced.

By the time an HR leader is evaluating a wellness platform, they don't need another article on why burnout is bad. They need:
— A comparison of implementation models
— A breakdown of what a rollout actually costs (in time, not just money)
— A case study from a company that looks like theirs

This is BOFU content. And most wellness brands don't have it.

Here's the 3-part test I use before briefing any wellness content:

1. Is this for someone still learning — or someone ready to decide?
2. Does it reduce friction for the buyer, or just educate the researcher?
3. Would an HR director bookmark this, or would they already know it?

If the answer to #3 is "they'd already know it," you're writing for search volume, not for sales.

BOFU content is the gap between traffic that looks good in a report and content that actually moves a deal.

What does your wellness content's bottom of funnel look like right now?
