# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-16

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in corporate wellness content — why most wellness brands stop at awareness and never give HR leaders the content they actually need to evaluate, justify, and decide.

---

Most corporate wellness content is built for one stage of the funnel: awareness.

HR leaders reading about "the ROI of employee wellbeing" are not at the top of the funnel. They already know wellness matters. The question they're actually asking is: how do I evaluate this, justify it to the CFO, and get leadership buy-in?

That's a BOFU problem. And most wellness brands don't answer it.

The gap shows up in three places:

**No comparison content.** HR leaders evaluating platforms need structured criteria. Most wellness brands publish benefits, not differentiators.

**No implementation content.** "How would this actually work at our company?" goes unanswered by most wellness blogs — and it's the exact question HR leaders bring into a demo.

**No CFO-facing content.** The person signing the contract isn't always the person doing the research. Brands that don't publish cost-justification frameworks lose deals they never knew they were in.

If your wellness content cluster ends at awareness, you're building an audience of people who know wellness matters. Not people who buy.

Map your last 10 posts to funnel stage. If nothing sits below MOFU, that's the gap.
