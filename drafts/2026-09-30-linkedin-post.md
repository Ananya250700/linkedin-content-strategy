# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-30

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams over-invest in awareness and never produce the content that actually helps HR leaders make a buying decision.

---

Most wellness content teams are creating for the wrong funnel stage.

They publish "why employee wellness matters" articles. They write "10 ways to support mental health at work." They produce infographics about burnout statistics.

Then they wonder why 20k monthly blog visits don't move the pipeline.

The gap is almost always the same: no content for the moment HR is actually ready to buy.

That's BOFU — bottom of funnel. The content that helps a CHRO justify budget, compare platforms, and explain ROI to their CFO.

The three pieces most wellness content teams are missing:
→ Honest comparison content ("How does X stack up against Y?")
→ ROI and cost-justification guides built for internal buy-in, not brand awareness
→ Implementation case studies that show what happens *after* the demo

Top-of-funnel content builds awareness. BOFU content closes deals.

If your wellness content is generating traffic but your sales team says leads are cold — this is usually why.

What does your content strategy look like at the bottom of the funnel?
