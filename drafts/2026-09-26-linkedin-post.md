**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is worth writing about (before you waste a content slot on it)

---

Most wellness content teams don't have a topic shortage. They have a prioritization problem.

The backlog is full. The calendar is packed. But half of what gets published is informational filler that neither ranks well nor moves an HR leader closer to a decision.

Here's the 3-part filter I use before greenlighting any wellness topic at Vantage Fit:

**1. Does this map to a real HR decision?**
Not "is burnout bad?" — but "what does an HR leader need to believe before they propose a burnout prevention program to leadership?" If the topic doesn't connect to a decision someone has to make, skip it.

**2. Is there a search angle we can actually own?**
High-volume wellness keywords are dominated by WebMD and Healthline. We target HR-specific intent — implementation, ROI, manager training, employee adoption — where a B2B wellness brand can compete.

**3. Does our POV make us more credible, not just more visible?**
If we sound like every other vendor on the topic, the piece isn't worth the effort. Every greenlit post should demonstrate what it looks like to actually build something — not just recommend it.

Most topics fail at least one filter. That's the point.

What does your content team use to decide what's worth writing?
