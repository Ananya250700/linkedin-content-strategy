# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-22

**Pillar:** Pillar 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap — most wellness content teams flood the top of the funnel and never write the content that actually closes decisions

---

Most corporate wellness content teams are producing the same thing: awareness-stage blogs about burnout, engagement trends, and mental health statistics.

They hit the top of the funnel well. Traffic looks healthy.

But HR leaders don't buy wellness platforms because they read a blog about burnout. They buy when something helps them justify the decision internally — to the CFO, to leadership, to themselves.

That's the BOFU gap. The content that answers:
- "What does ROI on a wellness program actually look like?"
- "How do I make the case for this budget to my CEO?"
- "What does implementation actually require from our team?"

Most wellness vendors skip this entirely. They produce thought leadership without ever writing the content that closes.

If you're mapping a wellness content cluster, build all three levels intentionally:
- TOFU: awareness (burnout stats, disengagement trends, industry research)
- MOFU: evaluation (what good programs look like, how to assess vendors)
- BOFU: decision support (ROI calculators, implementation guides, executive case studies)

Traffic without conversion is a visibility problem dressed as a content success.
