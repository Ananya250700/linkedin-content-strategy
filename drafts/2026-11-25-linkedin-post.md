# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-11-25

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most teams build awareness at scale while the decision-stage is empty, which is why content drives traffic but not demos.

---

Most wellness content teams are building awareness at scale while their BOFU is empty.

The funnel gap nobody talks about:

Top of funnel is crowded — "10 signs of burnout," "why wellness matters," productivity stats. HR leaders are reading this. They're not buying from it.

Middle of funnel exists — comparison guides, ROI frameworks, case studies. But they're generic. They address wellness ROI in theory, not in the context of an HR director at a 500-person company trying to get budget approved.

Bottom of funnel is almost always missing.

No content that helps HR leaders draft the internal business case. No content that answers "how do I know if this will work in my company?" No content that walks them through implementation so buying doesn't feel like a gamble.

The fix isn't more content. It's one BOFU piece done properly:

1. Identify the exact objection HR faces before sign-off
2. Write to that objection — not around it
3. Give them the language to sell wellness internally

If your content drives traffic but not demos, the problem is usually a missing BOFU — not a weak top-of-funnel.

What does your wellness content's BOFU look like?
