# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-17

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** What I look at before I optimize a blog post (it's not keywords first — it's conversion path, audience fit, and topical ownership)

---

Most content audits start and end with traffic data.

That's the wrong starting point.

When I'm deciding whether to optimize a Vantage Fit blog, I check three things before I open Search Console:

**1. What action does this URL drive?**
Traffic is a vanity metric if the page isn't built to do anything. Demo requests, newsletter signups, internal links to BOFU content — if there's no conversion path, more traffic just means more people leaving.

**2. Who's actually landing on this page?**
Wellness blog traffic skews consumer. HR decision-makers have different search intent than employees googling stress relief tips. If the audience doesn't match the buyer profile, ranking higher won't move anything downstream.

**3. Is this a topic we can own?**
Some wellness keywords are dominated by WebMD and Healthline. We're not competing there. I'm looking for the HR-specific angle — where a B2B wellness platform has a credibility advantage no general health site can replicate.

Only after those three checks do I look at keyword gaps and word count.

The blogs that have actually moved the needle didn't just rank — they ranked for the right audience and gave them a reason to stay.

What do you look at before you start an optimization?
