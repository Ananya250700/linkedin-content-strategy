# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-04

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** Most wellness content teams build for awareness and stop there — they never close the BOFU gap that converts HR leaders from interested to decided.

---

Most workplace wellness content lives at the top of the funnel and dies there.

You'll find hundreds of blogs explaining *why* burnout matters. Articles making the business case for mental health programs. Guides on building a wellness culture.

What you won't find: content that helps an HR leader actually choose a platform, run an internal pilot, or justify the spend to a CFO.

That's the BOFU gap — and it's why wellness vendors get traffic but not demos.

Here's the lens I use:

**TOFU** answers: "Is this a real problem?"
**MOFU** answers: "What are my options?"
**BOFU** answers: "Why this solution, why now, why us?"

Most wellness brands stop at MOFU. They explain the solution category without closing the decision gap.

The fix isn't more content. It's mapping what an HR leader needs to believe before they book a demo — and writing backwards from that moment.

A platform comparison guide. A pilot-launch checklist. An ROI breakdown HR can bring to a budget meeting. A case study with numbers, not just quotes.

That's what converts.

What BOFU content have you seen that actually moved you toward a decision?
