# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-09-05

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** Most wellness content teams run TOFU-heavy — here's the 3-layer funnel map that reveals where your content is actually failing to convert.

---

Most HR content teams have 30 blogs about "how to improve employee wellbeing."

Zero about how to actually choose a wellness platform.

That's not a content gap. That's a funnel gap.

In B2B wellness content, the default is TOFU — broad topics, high search volume, educational framing. It generates traffic. It doesn't generate pipeline. The layer most wellness content teams skip entirely is MOFU to BOFU: the content that helps an HR leader compare approaches, build an internal case, and justify a buying decision to their CFO.

A simple 3-layer audit for your content map:

→ **TOFU** — "What is employee wellness?" (Educates. Builds traffic. Attracts HR assistants.)
→ **MOFU** — "What should a corporate wellness program include?" (Qualifies intent. Attracts decision-shapers.)
→ **BOFU** — "How to evaluate a wellness platform for 500+ employees" (Drives shortlisting. Attracts buyers.)

If you have 20 TOFU posts for every 1 BOFU post, you're not building a content funnel. You're building an audience that never converts.

Map your last 10 wellness blogs against this. Where does your content actually sit?
