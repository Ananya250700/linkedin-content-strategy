# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-06-21

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most teams over-invest in awareness content and never build what converts an HR buyer who's already sold on wellness.

---

Most wellness content teams have a TOFU obsession.

They track sessions, celebrate "employee wellness" keyword rankings, and report traffic growth in monthly reviews.

Then they wonder why demo requests are flat.

The gap isn't awareness. HR decision-makers know wellness matters — they've read 50 articles that say so. What they don't have is content that helps them act: justify budget to a CFO, compare vendors, or build a business case that survives a leadership review.

BOFU wellness content looks like:
→ ROI frameworks with real numbers, not just "studies show"
→ Case studies written for the person signing the check, not the HR manager reading the blog
→ Comparison guides that answer *why you* — not just what you do
→ Implementation playbooks that lower the perceived switching risk

Most content teams skip this because BOFU content is harder to write, smaller in volume, and impossible to rank for at scale. There's no 10,000-search keyword for "how to convince my CFO to invest in wellness."

But that's exactly why it converts.

The question worth asking isn't "how do we get more traffic?" It's "where in the funnel are we losing HR buyers who are already sold on wellness?"

That's almost always where the real gap lives.
