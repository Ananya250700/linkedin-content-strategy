# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-06-17

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap: most wellness content teams have 20 top-of-funnel posts for every 1 decision-stage asset — then wonder why leads don't convert.

---

Most wellness content lives at the top of the funnel.

Trend reports. Awareness blogs. "Why employee wellness matters" pieces that rank well and convert nobody.

The BOFU gap is the real problem — and most teams don't catch it because traffic metrics look healthy.

Decision-stage content answers one question: *"Why this platform, not the other one?"*

HR leaders shortlisting vendors need:
→ Comparison guides that are actually honest (not disguised product pitches)
→ ROI frameworks with real benchmark data, not vanity numbers
→ Implementation case studies that show what went wrong and how it was fixed

Most wellness content teams have 20 TOFU posts for every 1 BOFU asset. Then they wonder why the sales team says leads aren't ready.

If you're a content leader in HR tech: audit your funnel. Count how many pieces directly address the question a CHRO asks in the final two weeks before signing.

That number will tell you everything.

What does your BOFU content look like right now — or does it even exist?
