# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-07

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** What I check before I optimize a blog post — it's a positioning problem before it's an SEO problem

---

Most B2B blogs don't need more SEO. They need a clearer decision.

Before I touch keyword data on any Vantage Fit blog, I ask three questions:

**1. Who is this actually for?**
Not the persona doc — the real person. Is this for the HR manager running a wellness pilot, or the CHRO making a budget case? They need completely different content.

**2. What does this post ask the reader to do next?**
If the only CTA is "book a demo," the post is doing the brand's job, not the reader's. Useful content answers a question AND moves someone forward.

**3. Is this post competing with itself?**
High-traffic posts often cannibalize each other. I've killed (or consolidated) posts that ranked for the same intent but were spread across 3 URLs.

Only after those three questions do I open Search Console.

Keyword gaps are easy to fix. A post that exists for the wrong reason — that's harder to rescue.

Most B2B content optimization gets treated as a technical problem. It's a positioning problem first.

What's your first check before you touch a piece of content?
