# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-10-02

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Your wellness blog is ranking. Your pipeline isn't moving. Here's why traffic intent and buyer intent aren't the same thing.

---

Your wellness content is ranking. Nobody's buying.

That's the quiet crisis in B2B wellness content right now.

Most HR tech brands invest in SEO, build out a blog, hit Page 1 for terms like "employee wellness programs" — and then wonder why demo requests aren't coming. The traffic looks healthy. The pipeline doesn't move.

Here's the problem: wellness content that ranks is usually written for Google, not for the HR leader staring at a Q4 board deck trying to justify a wellness budget. It answers "what is employee wellness?" when the actual buyer question is "how do I convince my CFO this is worth it?"

Traffic intent and buyer intent are not the same thing. Most wellness blogs are optimized for the former while being sold as the latter.

What shifts when you build for buyer intent:
— BOFU content that answers objections, not definitions
— Case studies framed around business outcomes, not feature lists
— Pillar pages that guide decisions, not just inform searches

Is your wellness content designed to educate everyone — or convert someone?
