# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-08-14

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Most wellness blogs drive traffic but don't convert — the TOFU trap in HR tech content

---

Most wellness blogs rank on Google. Almost none of them convert.

Here's why.

I've reviewed dozens of wellness content pieces — ours and competitors'. The ones getting traffic target "top 10 wellness tips" or "how to reduce employee burnout." Clean keywords. Decent backlinks. Good rankings.

But they weren't driving demo requests.

The problem is funnel position. Most wellness content is built for awareness — informational, educational, safe. But HR decision-makers who are close to evaluating a solution don't need more information. They need the ROI argument, a use case that mirrors their company size, or a framework they can take to their CHRO.

TOFU content without BOFU content is a traffic engine with no destination.

The fix isn't more content. It's mapping what you already have against the decision stages your buyer actually goes through — then writing the missing pieces.

One question I keep coming back to: are you writing for the HR manager who's just learning, or the CHRO who needs to justify budget? Those are different posts. Most wellness content teams are only writing one of them.

What does your wellness content funnel look like right now?
