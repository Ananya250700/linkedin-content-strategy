# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-05-29

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Why wellness blogs with strong traffic produce zero pipeline — and the content cluster structure that actually converts HR buyers

---

Your wellness blog has 10,000 monthly visitors. And zero demo requests from it.

That's not a traffic problem. That's a content strategy problem.

Most corporate wellness content is built to rank, not to convert. It targets informational keywords — "what is employee burnout," "signs of workplace stress" — attracts readers who are researching a problem, not buyers evaluating a solution, and stops there.

The result: impressive traffic dashboards. Empty pipelines.

The fix isn't publishing more content. It's building a cluster that moves people through a decision — from "I recognize this problem" to "I know what solving it looks like" to "I can build the internal business case."

That requires three types of content working together:

→ Awareness: Name the problem honestly, not optimistically
→ Consideration: Show what good looks like, with specifics
→ Decision: Give HR leaders language to justify the investment internally

Most wellness brands only publish awareness content. Then they wonder why their blog doesn't convert.

If your content is generating traffic but no intent signals, the problem isn't volume. It's where you're putting all the effort.

What does your content calendar look like across those three stages?
