# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-23

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Why wellness blogs drive traffic but not pipeline — the BOFU gap most wellness content teams ignore

---

Your wellness blog gets 10,000 monthly visits. Zero of them turned into a demo request last quarter.

That's not a traffic problem. It's a content alignment problem.

Most corporate wellness content is written to rank — not to convert. It answers "what is employee burnout" because the keyword has volume. But the HR leader reading that post already knows what burnout is. They came to your site because they're trying to decide whether to buy something.

The gap: informational content built for top-of-funnel traffic, with nothing that serves someone who's mid-decision.

What actually closes it:
— Replace "what is X" posts with "how to implement X in a 500-person org"
— Add a decision-layer section to your top-traffic posts: cost comparison, implementation timeline, what to tell your CFO
— Write at least one BOFU piece per cluster that speaks to the buying committee, not just the researcher

Traffic without conversion data is vanity. If your wellness content team isn't tracking assisted conversions and pipeline influence, the blog is a cost center — not a revenue lever.

What does your content-to-pipeline attribution actually look like right now?
