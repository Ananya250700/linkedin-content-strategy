# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-08-31

**Pillar:** Pillar 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** The traffic-vs-pipeline gap: why TOFU-heavy wellness content strategies produce rankings but not revenue

---

Most wellness brands have the same problem: high traffic, zero pipeline.

A post ranking #1 for "employee burnout statistics" is not the same as content that converts HR leaders into buyers.

The gap isn't the keywords. It's the funnel stage.

Wellness vendors pour resources into TOFU — educational content for searchers who are curious, not buying. HR leaders don't convert on awareness content. They convert when content names their specific problem and hands them a decision path.

The result: impressive traffic dashboards. Empty pipelines.

A simple stage check before any brief:

— TOFU: "What is burnout?" → educates, doesn't convert
— MOFU: "How do companies reduce burnout in shift work?" → signals buying intent
— BOFU: "What to look for in a wellness platform" → decision-ready

Most wellness content teams are heavy on TOFU and nearly empty at BOFU. That's where pipeline leaks — not the SEO strategy.

Before the next piece lands on your desk: which stage is it for? Is there a clear next step built into the close, or does it just end?

If you can't answer both, you're writing for traffic — not for buyers.
