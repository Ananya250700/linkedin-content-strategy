# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-11-15

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss: they build TOFU but skip BOFU, so HR buyers never get content that helps them actually decide.

---

Most corporate wellness content lives permanently at the top of the funnel. That's why it gets traffic but no pipeline.

HR leaders read wellness blogs when they're researching. Almost no wellness vendor creates content for when they're deciding.

The gap looks like this:

**TOFU:** "What is employee burnout?" — you have 50 of these.
**MOFU:** "How do we address burnout at scale?" — you have a few.
**BOFU:** "How do I evaluate whether this wellness platform will actually work for our org?" — almost nobody has this.

The BOFU content that moves HR buyers:

1. ROI frameworks tied to their actual metrics (cost per claim, absenteeism, turnover rate)
2. Implementation case studies — not press releases, real rollout stories with the friction included
3. Honest comparison guides that name category alternatives

If your wellness content plan doesn't include at least one BOFU piece per quarter, you're building awareness for a category, not pipeline for your product.
