# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-21

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** Why I retired a high-traffic blog — traffic without ICP fit is just noise

---

I retired a blog post that was pulling 4,000 monthly visits.

Not because it was outdated. Not because the keyword dried up.

Because it was bringing in the wrong people.

The post ranked well for a broad wellness term. The traffic looked great in GA4. But when I mapped the audience back to our ICP — HR leaders and People & Culture heads at mid-market companies — it didn't fit. The readers were students, HR generalists doing light research, people with zero buying intent.

Optimizing that piece further would have been busy work dressed up as strategy.

So I redirected its internal link equity to a BOFU cluster we were building and let the traffic go.

The lesson I took from it: high traffic on a B2B blog is only worth celebrating if you've first defined what the right reader looks like.

Before I touch any post for optimization now, I run three checks:
1. Does this reader match our ICP?
2. What decision does this content help them make?
3. Does the conversion path actually exist?

If any answer is unclear, the brief gets rewritten before the post gets touched.
