**Pillar:** Pillar 4 — Vantage Fit Work Amplification
**Angle:** Wellness programs fail at scale because the content was written for HQ, not frontline workers — surfaced while building Vantage Fit's manufacturing wellness implementation guide.

---

Your wellness portal has 80% enrollment. Engagement is at 12%.

Most HR teams respond by redesigning the program. The actual problem is usually the content — who it's written for, and when it shows up.

While building Vantage Fit's implementation guide for manufacturing companies, one pattern kept surfacing: manufacturing HR teams are running desk-worker wellness content on shift-worker populations. Same assets, different context, completely different result.

A frontline worker deciding whether to open a wellness app at the start of a 6 AM shift doesn't respond to the same framing as a remote employee browsing during open enrollment. The underlying wellness need might be identical. The content that reaches them is not.

This is why wellness programs "work" in pilot rollouts and quietly fail at scale. The pilot is almost always with HQ employees. Scale means frontline workers who were never the content's original audience.

If your engagement numbers are flat, before you redesign the program — audit what the content is actually saying, and to whom it was written.
