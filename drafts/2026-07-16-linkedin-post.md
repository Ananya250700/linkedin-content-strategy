# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-07-16

**Pillar:** 4 — Vantage Fit Work Amplification
**Angle:** Financial stress is the wellness issue most HR programs are designed to ignore — and it's quietly driving disengagement more than any other factor.

---

Most wellness programs treat financial stress as someone else's problem.

Not HR's. Not L&D's. Certainly not the company's.

So they invest in gym subsidies, meditation apps, and EAP counseling — and never ask why 60% of employees report that money concerns are their top source of stress at work. Or why that stress doesn't disappear when the yoga class ends.

Financial anxiety is a cognitive load issue before it's a financial one. An employee calculating whether rent clears payroll is not a focused employee — regardless of how many wellness benefits they're enrolled in.

I wrote a piece for Vantage Fit on building a financial wellness strategy that HR teams can actually act on — not investment advice or generic budgeting tips, but the structural levers companies have: earned wage access, financial literacy resources, transparent compensation practices, and benefit design that actually reduces financial friction.

The uncomfortable part: most organizations already have the tools. They just haven't connected them into a coherent strategy.
