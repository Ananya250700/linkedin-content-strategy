**Angle:** The manufacturing wellness gap: content covers physical health because that's where HR is comfortable — not where employees actually have needs.

---

The manufacturing workforce wellness conversation always starts in the same place: physical safety, ergonomics, occupational health.

That's the visible layer. It's also the layer that gets all the content.

Working on Vantage Fit's implementation guide for manufacturing wellness, the more interesting finding was the invisible layer: mental health resources and financial wellness tools had consistently higher engagement rates in manufacturing cohorts than in general corporate populations — but almost no wellness content for this sector addresses them.

The reason is simple and uncomfortable: wellness content follows where HR feels comfortable publishing, not where employees actually have gaps.

A factory worker with back pain gets content. The same worker anxious about shift instability or mounting medical costs gets a generic "EAP is available" footer.

The guide we built tries to close that gap — practical frameworks for HR managers in high-physical-demand environments who need to expand wellness beyond the obvious entry point.

If you work in HR for manufacturing, utilities, logistics, or similar sectors, this is the piece I'd want you to read before you plan your next wellness cycle.
