# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-09-29

**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** Before I optimize any blog post, I run it through a 3-question audit — not a keyword check.

---

Most content teams optimize for rankings. I optimize for the reader's next step.

When I get a brief to improve a Vantage Fit blog, my first move isn't to open a keyword tool. It's to ask three questions:

1. **Who is this actually for?** Not "HR professionals" — that's too broad. Is it a benefits manager running a pilot? A CHRO building the business case? The answer changes everything: the hook, the examples, the CTA.

2. **What decision is this post supposed to help them make?** If I can't answer that in one sentence, the post has no real job. Traffic without a downstream action is just vanity.

3. **Is the content honest enough to be useful?** B2B wellness content tends to be aspirational. The posts that actually help HR leaders are the ones that name the real friction — budget constraints, low participation rates, leadership skepticism. Sanitized content ranks. Honest content converts.

Only after I can answer all three do I look at the keyword data — and usually, the data just confirms what the answers already told me.

If your content team's optimization process starts with search volume, you're starting a step too late.
