# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-10-28

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — why wellness blogs drive traffic reports but not demo pipelines, and a 3-question audit to fix it.

---

Most wellness content teams have a traffic obsession. Not a lead obsession.

They win on "signs of employee burnout" — 8,000 monthly visits. But the HR leader who's 30 days from signing a vendor contract doesn't search that. She searches "best employee wellness platform for mid-sized companies."

And most wellness content teams don't have that page. Or they do, and it's buried, weak, and conversion-optimized for nothing.

This is the BOFU gap. It's why wellness blogs drive traffic reports but not demo pipelines.

Three questions I use to audit it:

1. What does your buyer search for when they're actively evaluating vendors?
2. Do you have content built around that intent — or just awareness content?
3. What's the CTA on your decision-stage pages? If it's "subscribe to our newsletter," something is wrong.

The uncomfortable truth: most wellness content strategies are built to impress content marketers, not convert HR buyers.

Traffic is vanity. Intent alignment is strategy.

What's your highest-converting blog? I'd bet it's not your most-trafficked one.
