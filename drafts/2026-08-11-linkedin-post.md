**Pillar:** 2 — Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
**Angle:** What I check before optimizing a blog post (search intent, funnel fit, and conversion path — not keyword rankings)

---

Most content teams start blog optimization with keyword rankings.

That's the wrong first step.

Before I touch a meta tag or rewrite a heading, I check three things:

**1. Is the search intent actually matched?**
A post ranking for "employee wellness programs" might be written for HR generalists when the real buyer is a CHRO. Google can rank it. It won't convert.

**2. Does the content have a logical next step?**
High-traffic wellness blogs often dead-end. No CTA that fits the reader's decision stage. A CHRO reading about burnout doesn't want a "request a demo" button — they want a framework or a benchmark to take back to leadership.

**3. What is this page actually doing in the funnel?**
Traffic without funnel context is noise. I map where the post sits — awareness, consideration, decision — and check whether the content matches the intent of someone at that stage.

Only after those three checks do I look at keyword gaps, heading structure, or internal links.

Optimization without this context is just redecorating a page that was never built to convert.
