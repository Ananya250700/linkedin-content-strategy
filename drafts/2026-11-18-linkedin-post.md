# LinkedIn Post Draft — 2026-11-18

**Pillar:** 3 — Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
**Angle:** The BOFU gap in wellness content — most teams build great awareness content but never close the funnel for HR decision-makers

---

Most wellness content teams are great at awareness. They're terrible at closing.

After two years of building content for Vantage Fit, the pattern is predictable: strong TOFU — solid traffic on "what is employee burnout," "signs of workplace stress," high-volume keywords. Then a cliff.

The BOFU layer is almost always missing or broken.

Here's why that matters: HR leaders evaluating a wellness platform already know wellness matters. They don't need another post about the business case. They need content that answers the questions they're afraid to ask their vendor:

→ What does implementation actually look like in a 500-person company?
→ How do I justify this spend to a skeptical CFO?
→ What do companies similar to mine actually measure?

A quick filter I use before writing any content:
1. Does this help an HR leader convince someone above them?
2. Does it address a real objection — not a convenient one?
3. Does it show what happens after go-live, not just before?

If the answer to all three is no, it belongs in awareness, not decision-stage content.

The gap isn't a content volume problem. It's a funnel architecture problem.
