# LinkedIn Draft — 2026-11-09

**Pillar:** 1 — Honest Takes on Wellness Content
**Angle:** Most wellness content is built to rank, not to convert — and the gap between traffic and pipeline is a strategy failure, not a volume problem.

---

Most wellness content teams are optimizing for the wrong metric.

High traffic ≠ pipeline. I've seen wellness blogs hit 50K monthly visits and generate almost no demo requests. Because the content was designed to rank, not to convert.

Here's what's usually happening:

The content answers awareness-stage questions — "what is employee burnout?" — but stops there. It never walks the HR leader toward a decision. No BOFU content. No bridge between "I understand the problem" and "here's how this platform solves it."

The fix isn't more content. It's better mapping.

Before writing another piece, ask three questions:
1. Where is this reader in their buying journey?
2. Does this post have a next step — a case study, a framework, a demo CTA — that matches their intent?
3. If this ranks #1, what actually happens next for the reader?

Traffic is vanity. Conversion paths are strategy.

If you work in wellness content or HR tech: what's the BOFU piece you wish existed but doesn't yet?
