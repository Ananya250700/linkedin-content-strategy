Most workplace wellness content is built for desk workers.

Step challenges. Mindfulness apps. Flexible work hours. Every solution assumes a laptop and a WiFi connection. For plant floor workers on rotating shifts, this content is almost completely useless.

I spent the last few weeks building out Vantage Fit's implementation guide for manufacturing wellness programs. The research kept surfacing the same pattern: HR teams in manufacturing know exactly what their workforce needs — better ergonomic support, shift-appropriate health check-ins, managers trained to recognize fatigue — but almost none of the mainstream wellness content speaks to that reality.

The guide breaks this down by environment type: light manufacturing, heavy industry, and shift-heavy operations. Each has a different implementation priority and a different gap between what's being offered and what workers actually need.
