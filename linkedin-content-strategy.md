# LinkedIn Content Strategy — Ananya Talukdar

## Goals (Priority Order — Next 90 Days)
1. Thought leadership in workplace wellness content and HR tech
2. Vantage Fit brand amplification
3. Personal brand building
4. Inbound career opportunities

## Core Positioning
**Who you are on LinkedIn:** B2B workplace wellness content strategist — uniquely positioned at the intersection of corporate law, SEO cluster strategy, and HR tech content. Nearly 2 years of hands-on experience building content for HR decision-makers at Vantage Fit.

**The differentiator:** Nobody else combines a legal background, B2B SaaS content operations, and a genuine understanding of why wellness content fails to convert.

## Core POV (The Stake in the Ground)
> *"Most workplace wellness content is too polished to feel real. Brands avoid uncomfortable truths about burnout, toxic work culture, and emotional exhaustion to stay safe and positive — but employees connect more with honest, human content than perfectly branded wellness messaging."*

**How to deploy this POV without alienating HR leaders:**
Challenge first → Framework second. Lead with the uncomfortable truth to earn credibility, then give HR leaders the implementation path to fix it. The POV earns attention; the framework earns trust and saves.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Honest Takes on Wellness Content
Posts that name what's broken in corporate wellness content — polished messaging, wellness washing, performative programs. Draws from real observations in the field.

**Example angles:**
- Why your wellness blog drives traffic but not demo requests
- The difference between wellness content that performs and wellness content that converts
- What HR leaders actually want to read vs. what most wellness vendors publish

### 2. Behind-the-Process: B2B Content Strategy
Documenting your actual workflow — how you build a content cluster, decide what to optimize vs. retire, write for HR decision-makers without sounding like a vendor.

**Example angles:**
- How I map a content cluster for a B2B wellness platform
- What I look at before I optimize a blog (it's not just keywords)
- Why I killed a high-traffic post — and what I did instead

### 3. Frameworks for HR Content Leaders
Original frameworks rooted in real work — not generic advice. Structured, scannable, saveable.

**Example angles:**
- A 3-part filter for deciding if a wellness topic is worth writing about
- The funnel gap most wellness content teams miss (BOFU)
- How to brief a wellness blog that actually helps HR make a decision

### 4. Vantage Fit Work Amplification
Selective sharing of published work with a POV — not just "new blog out." Add your take, the insight behind the piece, or what the data showed.

**Format:** Share link + 2–3 lines of original commentary that gives a reason to click.

## Content Format Mix
| Format | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Text-only POV posts | 2x/week | Thought leadership, reach |
| Carousel / framework posts | 1x/week | Saves, authority |
| Blog amplification posts | 1x/week | Vantage Fit traffic |
| Engagement / comments | Daily | Network growth |

## Voice and Tone
- Authoritative but not corporate
- Honest and direct — name the uncomfortable thing
- Never motivational or influencer-style
- Short sentences. No filler. Every line earns its place.

## What to Avoid
- Generic wellness tips ("drink water, take breaks")
- Vanity posting — sharing content without a POV
- Over-polished brand voice that contradicts the core POV
- Posting without engaging — LinkedIn rewards conversation

## 90-Day Milestones
- **Month 1:** Establish posting rhythm, publish core POV post, document 1 behind-the-process piece
- **Month 2:** Test carousel format, build engagement in HR and content marketing communities
- **Month 3:** Evaluate what's resonating, double down on top-performing pillar, refine positioning
